## 1. Executive Summary & Timeline Distinction (LOW 1)

On `2026-11-22`, infra edge node `ns1.bitecho.org` (1 GB GCP `e2-micro`) experienced severe memory pressure under unthrottled hot-cache retention (`128 MiB` default) compounded by SSH brute-force connection churn and verbose raw byte-array artifact logging.

Two distinct duration boundaries are tracked explicitly (closing Axiom Stage 1 **LOW 1**):

1. **`ns1` Host Unreachable Window:** `88.6 min` (`05:09:25 CST` → `06:38:02 CST` / `11:09:25Z` → `12:38:02Z`) — `ns1` failed `/statusz` and HTTPS/relay connections until operator recovery restored the node.
2. **End-to-End Single-Relay Client Recovery Window:** `94.7 min` (`05:09:25 CST` → `06:44:08 CST` / `11:09:25Z` → `12:44:08Z`) — single-relay clients pinned solely to `ns1` did not observe full end-to-end sync recovery until `06:44:08 CST` (`+6.1 min` post-node-recovery reconnect/catch-up tail).

Throughout the entire window, `ns2` and `ns3` maintained healthy consensus finality and continued advancing epochs without interruption.
