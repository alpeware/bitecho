## 1. Blameless Summary

On 2026-11-22, devnet consensus finalization stalled at finalized epoch `85690` for approximately 20 minutes (`~1,200,000 ms`), exceeding the 24-hour six-nines error budget by `13,888.9×` (`463.0×` 30-day budget, `38.05×` annual budget; `:six-nines-met? false`).

1. **Root Cause (`F-EPOCH-1`):** After skipped epochs caused `tip-depth < tip-epoch`, an older-parent late fork block arrived whose parent was already in `:executed-finalized-blocks`. `bitecho.streamlet.core/record-block-depth` consulted `:executed-finalized-blocks` before cached `:block-depths` and seeded the parent's depth from `:last-executed-finalized-epoch`, producing a mathematically impossible `depth > epoch` entry and deadlocking `longest-notarized-parent?` selection at epoch `85690`.
2. **Why Rollback-First SOP Could Not Resolve `F-EPOCH-1`:** Once the poisoned `depth > epoch` entry was persisted in validator state, rolling back to the prior bundle left the corrupted `:block-depths` entry intact. Running `repair-corrupt-block-depths` (`b52a95c`, reclassified strictly as `:provisional-incident-mitigation`) was required to clear the poisoned depth and restore liveness.
3. **Compounding Observability & Client Blind Spots:**
   - `bitecho.observability.core/record-startup-event` reset `:active-stall-started-ms` to `nil` on deploy restarts, erasing ~96% of the outage from `sla/latest.edn` (`45,766 ms` recorded instead of `~1,200,000 ms`).
   - Light-node `auto-sync` reported `ok: true, synced: true` on `2f+1` relay agreement over frozen epoch `85690` without emitting a stall diagnostic.

## 2. Permanent Prevention (BIP-019 Draft 2)

1. **S2 — Streamlet Depth Hardening (`bitecho.streamlet.core`):**
   - Maintain immutable `:executed-root-depths` (`{block-hash true-depth}`) whenever blocks are finalized/executed and preserve it across `prune-streamlet-state` (`> 256` epochs), `rebind-restored-state`, and `ingest-verified-ancestors`.
   - Consult `:block-depths` then `:executed-root-depths` before any fallback; never seed depth from `:epoch` or `:last-executed-finalized-epoch`.
   - Emit counted Rule 5 `:corrupt-block-depth-repaired` diagnostics in `repair-corrupt-block-depths` and surface the counter in `/statusz` and `digest.edn`.
2. **S3 — Stall-Masking Fixes (`bitecho.observability.core`, `bitecho.light-node.core`, `bitecho.node-main`):**
   - Preserve `:active-stall-started-ms` across restarts in `record-startup-event`.
   - Detect frozen `finalized-epoch` beyond the stall threshold in `evaluate-auto-sync-stall` / `run-auto-sync-tick!`, emitting counted Rule 5 `:diagnose {:reason :finalized-epoch-stalled}` and surfacing `:stalled? true` in `ctl status`.
